## AI-Assisted Requirements Candidates – Clinic Appointment System

### Elicitation Notes

- Manual spreadsheet-based scheduling
- Doctors require buffer time between appointments
- Patients want SMS/email reminders
- IT team requires audit logs
- Medical details cannot be shared via SMS

### Key Problems Summary

The current clinic appointment system faces several critical challenges: inefficient manual scheduling processes, lack of automated patient communication, insufficient time management for healthcare providers, missing compliance and audit capabilities, and privacy concerns around patient data transmission.

### Requirements Candidates

| **ID** | **Requirement** | **Type** | **Priority (MoSCoW)** | **Acceptance Criteria** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| REQ-001 | System shall provide automated appointment scheduling interface | Functional | Must Have | Users can book, modify, and cancel appointments without manual intervention; 95% of bookings complete in under 2 minutes |
| REQ-002 | System shall automatically allocate buffer time between appointments based on doctor preferences | Functional | Must Have | Configurable buffer periods (5-30 min) applied to all appointments; no double-booking occurs |
| REQ-003 | System shall send automated SMS and email reminders to patients | Functional | Must Have | Reminders sent 24 hours and 2 hours before appointment; delivery confirmation received; 90% delivery success rate |
| REQ-004 | System shall maintain comprehensive audit logs of all user actions | Non-Functional | Must Have | All CRUD operations logged with timestamp, user ID, and action details; logs retained for minimum 7 years |
| REQ-005 | System shall not include medical details in SMS notifications | Constraint | Must Have | SMS contains only appointment time, location, and generic reminder text; no PHI or diagnosis information transmitted |
| REQ-006 | System shall comply with HIPAA privacy and security standards | Constraint | Must Have | All data encrypted in transit and at rest; access controls implemented; BAA agreements in place with vendors |
| REQ-007 | System shall provide real-time calendar synchronization for doctors | Functional | Should Have | Calendar updates reflect within 5 seconds; integration with common calendar platforms (Google, Outlook) |
| REQ-008 | System shall generate reports on appointment utilization and no-show rates | Functional | Should Have | Reports available on-demand and scheduled; include metrics on occupancy, cancellations, and no-shows |
| REQ-009 | System shall support patient self-service portal for appointment management | Functional | Should Have | Patients can view, reschedule, and cancel appointments; secure authentication required |
| REQ-010 | System shall maintain 99.5% uptime during business hours | Non-Functional | Must Have | Measured monthly; scheduled maintenance outside business hours; SLA penalties for violations |

### Risk Analysis

- **Integration Complexity:** Integrating with existing EHR systems may require significant technical effort and vendor cooperation
- **User Adoption:** Staff and patients accustomed to manual processes may resist digital transition
- **Data Privacy Compliance:** Ensuring HIPAA compliance across all notification channels requires careful implementation
- **SMS Delivery Reliability:** Third-party SMS providers may experience outages affecting reminder delivery
- **Buffer Time Configuration:** Balancing doctor preferences with clinic efficiency may create scheduling conflicts

### Dependencies

- REQ-003 depends on REQ-005 (reminder system must respect privacy constraints)
- REQ-007 depends on REQ-001 (calendar sync requires automated scheduling foundation)
- REQ-009 depends on REQ-001 and REQ-006 (self-service requires scheduling system and security)
- REQ-008 depends on REQ-004 (reporting requires comprehensive data logging)
- All requirements depend on REQ-006 (HIPAA compliance is foundational)

### Prioritization Framework (MoSCoW)

**Must Have:** Core scheduling automation, buffer time management, compliant notifications, audit logging, privacy constraints, HIPAA compliance, and system availability

**Should Have:** Calendar integration, reporting capabilities, and patient self-service portal

**Could Have:** Advanced analytics, mobile app, waitlist management, integration with billing systems

**Won't Have (this release):** Telemedicine integration, AI-powered scheduling optimization, multi-location support
